How limits work

  • Alert – log an event and show a toast when the app goes over its limit.
  • Trim – push the app's idle memory back to Windows. Safe; the app keeps running but may re-grow.
  • Hard cap – Windows enforces a working-set ceiling (limit ÷ processes). The app pages instead of growing; no crash.
  • Terminate – end every process of the app after it stays over for N checks (2 s each). Unsaved work is lost.
